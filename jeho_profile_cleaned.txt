ABOUT

Hi, I am Jeho. I am 20 years old, currently studying BS in Computer Science at DMMMSU-SLUC, located in Consolacion, Agoo, La Union.

I chose Computer Science because I discovered my passion for programming during 10th grade. At the same time, I also had a dream of becoming an airline pilot someday, but I did not pursue flying school because it is very expensive. Aside from that, I am a hardworking person with a strong vision for my future, and I always try to keep moving forward.

I am originally from Bangued, Abra, specifically Barangay Dangdangla, Sitio Skwela — that is my hometown, where I grew up. I studied at Dangdangla Elementary School during my primary/elementary years. During my secondary years, from 2018 to 2024, I studied at Abra High School. In 2022, I took GAS (General Academic Strand) as my specialization — I originally wanted to take STEM, but I ran out of slots.

For my tertiary education, I started my BSCS degree in 2024 in Agoo, La Union at DMMMSU-SLUC. I moved out of my hometown to step out of my comfort zone, and so far it has been a good decision for me — the teaching quality here is very good, "walang palya" (never disappoints).

In the future, I am planning to take a master's degree in Computer Science, and if given the chance, I will also try to get an airline pilot license.
